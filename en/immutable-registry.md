<p class="hub-lead"><span class="hub-tag">Idea under study</span> A registry that nobody can quietly alter and anyone can verify: it proves that a document, a product or an action existed at a given moment and has not been changed.</p>

## How it works, in short
The file is never uploaded. Its fingerprint (hash) is computed and only that is written to a public, decentralized registry, with date and time. To verify, anyone recomputes the file's fingerprint: if it matches, the file is the original. Change a single comma and it no longer matches.

## What can be done: list of services
### Documents and evidence
- **Timestamping of documents**: contracts, private agreements, minutes, quotes, expert reports.
- **Proof of authorship and priority** for creators: texts, photos, music, graphics, designs, code. Useful when you need to show who created what and when.
- **Digital evidence**: keeping the fingerprint of screenshots, emails, chats, web pages and access logs.
- **Document versions**: a history of every change, to know which version was in force and when.
- **Public verification with a QR code**: anyone checks authenticity from a web page, with no account.

### Certificates and credentials
- **Diplomas, certificates and courses** that an employer can verify in seconds.
- **Professional licences and certifications**, with expiry and revocation visible.
- **Tickets, passes and cards** that cannot be forged.

### Product traceability
- **Food supply chain and Made in Italy**: batch, origin, steps and checks.
- **Anti-counterfeiting** for fashion, luxury, wine and crafts: a certificate of authenticity tied to each item.
- **Digital maintenance logbook** for cars, boats, machinery, systems and appliances.
- **Preparation for the digital product passport** planned by European rules: timing and obligations to be checked for each sector.

### Companies and compliance
- **Immutable audit log**: access, interventions, quality checks, workplace safety.
- **Environmental and social reporting (ESG)** with data that cannot be changed afterwards.
- **Software integrity**: fingerprints of releases, builds and dependencies.
- **Archive of consents and notices**, with proof of when and what was accepted.

### Construction and real estate
- **Site work history**: progress reports, dated photos, tests, material certificates.
- **Property file**: documents, works and compliance in one verifiable registry.

### Automatic contracts
- **Smart contracts** for conditional payments: money is released when a verified event happens.
- **Escrow** between two parties with no intermediary.
- **Signature and proof of agreement** on an exact text, with a certain date.

### Transparency
- **Donations and funds**: public tracking of income and spending for associations and projects.
- **Votes and assemblies**: a verifiable record of votes (condominiums, associations, cooperatives).
- **Prizes, rankings and competitions** with rules and results that cannot be altered.

## What customers really look for
From the sources we reviewed, the real demand is not "a blockchain": it is saving the time and cost of notaries and paperwork, reducing fraud and having evidence that holds up in a dispute. The sectors cited most are legal, finance, healthcare, real estate, intellectual property and production chains. Competitors already exist, so we need a clear niche: for example Italian small businesses, professionals and creators, with a simple interface in Italian and no technical jargon.

## Legal value: what we know and what must be checked
- In Italy, storing a document on distributed ledger technology produces the effects of electronic time validation under the EU eIDAS regulation (art. 8-ter, law 12/2019).
- Only **qualified** time validation carries a presumption of accurate date and data integrity. It requires technical standards and a qualified provider: a lawyer must confirm which route to take.
- The new eIDAS 2 adds electronic ledgers as a trust service: worth following.
- Evidential value in court varies case by case: we do not promise it.
- **Privacy (GDPR)**: no personal data on the registry, only fingerprints. No content, no names.
- Not everything needs a blockchain: if an ordinary database is enough, we tell the customer.

## How we could earn
Monthly subscription for companies and professionals, pay-per-stamp for individuals, API access for those who already have management software, and a white-label version under the client's brand. Prices are not set: we decide after validation.
