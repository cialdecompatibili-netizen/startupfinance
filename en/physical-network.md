<p class="hub-lead"><span class="hub-tag">Idea under study</span> Real things (parking spots, drones, charging points, machines, parcels) identified by a QR code or a sensor, paid automatically, leaving a proof nobody can alter afterwards.</p>

## How we read the idea
"Quad core" and "quadcode" are read here as **quadcopter drones** and **QR codes**. If you meant something else, we will fix the page.

## The scheme: three pieces
1. **Physical object**: a parking spot, a drone, a charging point, a machine.
2. **Identity and sensor**: a QR code, NFC tag or sensor saying "it's me" and "this happened".
3. **Registry and payment**: the event is recorded with a certain date and, if needed, an automatic contract triggers the payment.

## Parking
- **Bookable, phone-payable parking spots**: open, park, pay only for the time used.
- **Shared private parking**: owners of an unused garage or yard rent it by the hour (condominiums, hotels, shops, companies after hours).
- **Proof of parking**: entry and exit times recorded, useful in disputes over fines or damage.
- **Dynamic pricing**: higher when demand is high, lower when the spot is empty.
- **Subscriptions and permits** for residents, staff and guests, expiring automatically.
- **Charging points**: pay per kWh with a verifiable receipt.
- **Warning**: a license plate is personal data. Only its fingerprint is stored, never the plain plate, and a privacy assessment comes first.

## Drones (quadcopters)
- **Inspections**: roofs, solar panels, antennas, pylons, bridges, warehouses, construction sites. Flight and photos are recorded with a certain date, so the report cannot be changed later.
- **Surveys and maps**: land, quarries, building progress, agriculture.
- **Precision agriculture**: crops, irrigation, pests.
- **Light deliveries** to remote areas: medicines, spare parts, documents. Harder because of flight rules.
- **Photo and video** for real estate, tourism and events, with an authenticity certificate.
- **Flight and maintenance log** of the drone: hours, batteries, checks, pilot. Needed for insurance and authorizations.
- **"Drone on demand"**: the client requests a service, a licensed pilot performs it, payment is released when the report is delivered.
- **Pilot network**: licensed freelancers receive jobs and payments from one platform.

## Connected objects with QR or NFC
- **Maintenance logbook** for machines, lifts, systems, extinguishers, boats.
- **Checks and patrols**: the guard or technician scans the tag, proving they were there.
- **Parcels and deliveries**: every handover recorded from sender to receiver.
- **Rental** of equipment, bikes, scooters and tools with automatic unlocking and payment.
- **Authenticity** of valuables: watches, bags, instruments, wines.
- **Site equipment and safety**: who used what, when, with which checks.

## How we could earn
A fee on every booking or payment, a subscription for parking and fleet managers, sale or rental of tags and sensors, a fee for the maintenance registry and a percentage on drone jobs. Prices are not set: after validation.

## What customers look for
From the sources we reviewed, the most concrete demand is for drone inspection, surveys, logistics and smart parking. The immutable registry is not the product: it is the part that builds trust in payments, reports and responsibility. Many sources are from companies selling technology or token projects, so we do not treat them as market proof: we look for proof with real customers.

## Rules to check before starting
- **Drones**: operator registration, flight categories and authorizations under European and ENAC rules. No licence, no commercial flights.
- **Parking**: licences, municipal rules, insurance, liability for damage and theft.
- **Privacy (GDPR)**: plates, video and location are personal data. Only fingerprints go on the registry.
- **Payments**: an authorized provider is required. We never hold customers' money ourselves.
- **Tokens and cryptocurrencies**: not needed to start and we avoid them. Payment is in euros; the blockchain is only for proofs.

## First version we propose
A single field test: one private parking spot (an unused yard or garage) with a QR code, phone booking and payment, and a dated receipt. Then we measure whether anyone uses and pays for it, as in [how we validate]({{ '/en/how-we-validate/' | relative_url }}). In parallel, one drone service: roof inspection with a certified report.
