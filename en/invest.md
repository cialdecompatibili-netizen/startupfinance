<p class="hub-lead">Every startup in the project sits in one of three stages. The earlier you join, the more you risk and the more you can earn if the idea succeeds. The later you join, the less you risk and the more limited the potential. Here is the full ladder and your options at each stage.</p>

## The ladder of stages

<div class="hub-grid">
<div class="hub-card">
<span class="hub-tag">Stage 1</span>
<p><strong>Idea</strong></p>
<p><strong>Risk: highest. Potential: highest.</strong> Only the project exists: no customers, no proof that it works. Value is still low, so for the same sum or the same work the percentage is the highest.</p>
<p><strong>How you join:</strong> with your time or your capital, in exchange for a percentage of the project.</p>
</div>
<div class="hub-card">
<span class="hub-tag">Stage 2</span>
<p><strong>Under validation</strong></p>
<p><strong>Risk: high, falling. Potential: high.</strong> We talk to real people, build the smallest version and measure whether anyone uses or pays for it. Value starts to be measurable.</p>
<p><strong>How you join:</strong> with time or capital, in exchange for a percentage. For the same contribution, the stake tends to be lower than in stage 1.</p>
</div>
<div class="hub-card">
<span class="hub-tag">Stage 3</span>
<p><strong>Validated</strong></p>
<p><strong>Risk: lower, not zero. Potential: more limited.</strong> The idea works: there is real data, such as customers who use or pay. Value is higher and easier to verify.</p>
<p><strong>How you join:</strong> as an employee with a regular contract and salary, or by buying a stake at a higher value.</p>
</div>
</div>

After stage 2 comes a **decision**: continue (stage 3), change direction or shut down. If we shut down, the capital and time put in during stages 1 and 2 may be lost. How we reach the decision is explained in [How we validate]({{ '/en/how-we-validate/' | relative_url }}).

## Summary

| Stage | Risk | Potential | How you join | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea | Highest | Highest | Time or capital | Percentage of the project |
| 2. Under validation | High, falling | High | Time or capital | Percentage of the project |
| 3. Validated | Lower | More limited | Employee or buying a stake | Salary, or a stake at a higher value |

Higher potential is **not a guaranteed gain**: many ideas do not succeed. The earlier you join, the more your contribution counts, but the more likely it is that you will not get it back.

## The two paths in stages 1 and 2

**With your capital: you buy a percentage.** Pick a startup from the [list]({{ '/en/startups/' | relative_url }}) and offer a sum. The percentage depends on the project's value at that moment and on the amount: we define it together, case by case.

**With your time: you earn a percentage.** Developer, designer, marketer, salesperson, lawyer or other: contribute your work and receive a percentage of the project. Role, hours, goals and stake are written down before you begin.

## What must always be clear
- **Percentage**: it is not fixed; it depends on the startup's value, the sum or work contributed and the stage.
- **Written agreement**: no stake counts as assigned without a signed contract.
- **Legal form**: buying shares, capital increase, work as a contribution or an employment contract must be checked with an accountant or a lawyer.
- **Verified data**: for every startup we publish the stage and results with the source, with no invented numbers.

<div class="hub-box">Investing in early-stage startups carries a high risk, including the total loss of capital, and time spent may bring no return.
