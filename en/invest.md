## How it works
<ol class="hub-steps">
<li>Pick the startup you like from the <a href="{{ '/en/startups/' | relative_url }}">list</a>.</li>
<li>Write to us with the form below and tell us whether you want to join with capital or with time.</li>
<li>We reply with the available data: status, team, validation results.</li>
<li>If the interest is mutual, we define percentage, terms and legal form in writing, with a professional's help.</li>
</ol>

## What must always be clear
- **Percentage**: it is not fixed; it depends on the startup's value, the sum or work contributed and the stage of the project.
- **Written agreement**: no stake counts as assigned without a signed contract.
- **Legal form**: how you join a company (buying shares, capital increase, work as a contribution) must be checked with an accountant or a lawyer.
- **Time invested**: clear role and goals, so the percentage matches the work done.

<div class="hub-box">Investing in early-stage startups carries a high risk, including the total loss of capital, and time spent may bring no return. We do not promise returns and this page is not a public offering of financial instruments: it is an invitation to get in touch and evaluate a stake together.</div>
